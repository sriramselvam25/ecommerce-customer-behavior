# Interview Walkthrough

**Problem:** leadership needs one view of customer value, channel performance and growth quality.

**Approach:** I defined business KPIs, generated reproducible synthetic transactions, modelled dimensions around a central performance fact, transformed data in Power Query and created DAX for revenue, AOV, margin, YoY and target attainment.

**Design:** four pages progress from executive summary to customer segmentation, channel/product diagnosis and retention actions. Cross-filtering and drill-through preserve analytical context.

**Why it matters:** the solution demonstrates that I can move from business requirements through modelling and BI development to recommendations, rather than only creating charts.
