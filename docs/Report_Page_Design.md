# Report Page Design — Customer Growth

## 1 Executive Overview
Top KPI ribbon: Revenue, Active Customers, AOV, Repeat/Retention proxy, Gross Margin, YoY Growth. Main canvas: monthly Revenue + Customers timeline; segment contribution; channel share; compact insight rail.

## 2 Customer Segmentation
RFM-style segment bubble visual, segment revenue bars, value distribution, customer detail table. Selection persists until cleared and cross-highlights the page.

## 3 Channel & Product Performance
Channel/category matrix, contribution waterfall, category treemap and margin-vs-revenue scatter. Tooltip shows Revenue, Orders, AOV, Margin and target variance.

## 4 Retention & Growth Drivers
Cohort-style retention heatmap, repeat behavior timeline, at-risk segment contribution and growth-driver decomposition. End with three action cards: Protect, Grow, Recover.
